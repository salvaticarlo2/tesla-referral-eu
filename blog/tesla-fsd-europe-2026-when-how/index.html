Netherlands First, Then Seven More

After a missed March target, the Dutch vehicle authority RDW granted Tesla type approval for FSD Supervised under UN Regulation 171 on April 10, 2026, the first in Europe. Lithuania and Estonia followed in May, Denmark and Belgium in June, and Slovenia, Czechia and Croatia in September.

Other member states can adopt the Dutch approval nationally, but an EU-wide decision needs a qualified majority at the Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles: at least 15 of 27 states representing 65% of the EU population.

In March 2026, Tesla publicly demonstrated FSD Supervised navigating busy Dutch city centre traffic, handling roundabouts, cyclist-heavy intersections, pedestrian crossings, and dense urban road conditions. The demo was a key step in the approval process, showing that FSD performs safely in Europe's complex driving environment.

What Is Tesla FSD Supervised?

Tesla's Full Self-Driving (Supervised) is Tesla's most advanced driver assistance system. It handles steering, acceleration, and braking across highways and city streets, including traffic lights, stop signs, roundabouts, and complex intersections.

The key word is supervised. Despite the name, FSD is not fully autonomous. The driver must remain attentive and ready to take control at any time. In Europe, Tesla renamed the separate highway feature Navigate on Autopilot to Navigate on Autosteer (software 2026.2.9); FSD (Supervised) keeps its name in the Dutch configurator.

Under the hood, FSD uses Tesla's camera-based vision system powered by the FSD computer chip. In the US, FSD has been available since 2020, with over a billion miles of driving data collected. That data now benefits the European rollout.

How Much Will FSD Cost in Europe?

Tesla FSD Supervised is a paid add-on. What we saw in Tesla's configurators in early October 2026:

  • Netherlands: €99 per month, after a one-month trial
  • Germany: €99 per month listed, feature not yet available
  • One-time purchase: not shown in the Dutch or German configurator

Pricing and options change often; check your market's configurator before ordering.

One important note: FSD is tied to the vehicle, not your Tesla account. If you sell your car, the next owner keeps FSD access. Subscription-based FSD can be cancelled at any time.

FAQ — Tesla FSD Europe 2026

Is Tesla FSD available in Europe in 2026?

Yes, in some countries. As of October 3, 2026, eight EU countries allow FSD Supervised: the Netherlands (April 10), Lithuania (May), Estonia (May), Denmark (June), Belgium (June), Slovenia (September), Czechia (September 21) and Croatia (September 29). It runs on HW4 (AI4) cars only. An EU-wide vote was not on the October 6 agenda and is not expected before December 2026.

When is FSD coming to Germany, France, and other EU countries?

There is no confirmed date. Germany, France, Italy and Spain have not adopted the Dutch approval; France ran its own road tests in September 2026. An EU-wide vote is not expected before December 2026.

How much does Tesla FSD cost in Europe?

In the Netherlands, Tesla's configurator offered FSD (Supervised) at €99 per month after a one-month trial (October 3, 2026), and we did not see a one-time purchase option. In Germany the same package is listed at €99 per month while the feature itself is not yet available.

Is my Tesla compatible with FSD?

Only Hardware 4 (AI4) cars, fitted to new Teslas since around late 2023, are covered by the European approval. HW3 cars do not get FSD Supervised in Europe; Tesla's reduced FSD v14 Lite for HW3 was rolling out in North America and South Korea in September 2026, with no European date.

Which EU country gets Tesla FSD first?

The Netherlands, on April 10, 2026. Lithuania, Estonia, Denmark, Belgium, Slovenia, Czechia and Croatia followed by the end of September.
